Privacy Policy

Draft privacy policy for the Kinetic marketing site at flexkinetic.com.

Draft placeholder. This is a standard pre-launch marketing-site policy, not a final legal document. Counsel will review it before Kinetic relies on it or makes launch claims.

What this covers

This draft describes the public marketing pages at flexkinetic.com only. It does not describe a trainer dashboard, client texting, or any logged-in Kinetic product. Those need their own policy, reviewed by counsel, before launch.

What this site collects

These pages are static. They do not include an account form, a payment form, or an analytics script. The site does not ask you to create a profile.

Booking

If you book a fit call, your browser opens Cal.com. Information you submit there is collected by Cal.com under its own privacy policy. The host can see the details you enter on that booking page so the conversation can take place.

Hosting logs

The service that hosts flexkinetic.com may keep ordinary server logs — for example IP address, browser type, and the page requested — to deliver and protect the site. This draft does not set a retention period. Counsel will.

Cookies

The marketing pages do not set their own advertising or analytics cookies. If you open the Cal.com booking page, Cal.com may set cookies under its own policy.

Selling personal information

This marketing site is not set up to sell personal information. A formal “do not sell or share” notice, if one is required, will be added when counsel reviews this draft.

Children

The site is written for trainers. It is not directed at children.

Contact and changes

A mailing address and a dedicated privacy contact will be added after counsel review. Until then, the public site is flexkinetic.com. Kinetic may replace this draft before launch.
